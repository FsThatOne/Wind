- Water shore edges must align perfectly across cell boundaries to form 2x2 or larger pools.

16-bit pixel art wuxia RPG, clean readable pixel edges, restrained ink-wash influenced palette, no chunky outlines, soft muted colors, authentic ancient Chinese water-town feel. IMPORTANT isometric tile drawing rules:
- Each tile is a diamond (rotated square) shape filling the cell, point up/down/left/right.
- The diamond occupies roughly the full width and full height of its cell, touching near the cell edges.
- Land tiles fill the entire diamond with their texture.
- Water shore tiles have water in the specified corner/edge area and land in the remaining area, with a smooth curved shoreline transition (not a sharp diagonal cut).
- Shorelines should look like natural earth meeting water, with darker wet pixels at the water edge.

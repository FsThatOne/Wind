## Reproduction Steps

**Preconditions**: Start the Fengzhi vertical slice and enter a combat state where the move selection panel is visible.

1. Open or observe the player move selection panel.
2. Inspect the available actions.
3. Hover the mouse over one or more move entries.
4. Inspect unavailable move/action states.

**Expected Result**:

- The move selection panel shows 6 equipped moves plus `调息` and `使用道具`.
- The move selection panel does not show `普通攻击`.
- Hovering or focusing a move shows a preview card.
- The preview card shows relationship / counter hints, but no damage prediction.
- Unavailable actions show a clear disabled state and reason, such as insufficient neixi, sealed xinfa, or no usable item.

**Actual Result**:

- `普通攻击` does not appear.
- `使用道具` does not appear.
- Hovering the mouse over moves shows no additional content.
- Unavailable reasons are not visible.
- No preview card is visible.

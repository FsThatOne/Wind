## Reproduction Steps

**Preconditions**: Enter a combat state where the move selection panel can be opened.

1. Open the move selection panel.
2. Without clicking any move, press keyboard Down/Up to switch moves.
3. Click the first move manually.
4. Press keyboard Down to navigate through moves.
5. Continue pressing Down after reaching the last move.

**Expected Result**:

- Move panel opens with focus on the first available action.
- Keyboard/gamepad navigation works immediately without requiring a mouse click.
- Pressing Down on the last action wraps to the first action.

**Actual Result**:

- Keyboard navigation does not work immediately after entering the panel.
- The first move must be clicked manually before keyboard navigation works.
- Pressing Down on the last action does not wrap to the first action.
